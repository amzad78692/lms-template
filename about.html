Our Mission

Education Without Borders

We believe education is the most powerful tool for changing lives. EduLearn was built to break down the barriers — cost, geography, language, and time — that have historically kept quality education out of reach.

From our AI-powered learning paths to our community of peer mentors, every feature we build is designed with one question in mind: "Does this help a learner succeed?"

Accessible Learning

Free courses, flexible schedules, and multi-language support ensure nobody is left behind.

Quality First

Every course is peer-reviewed, industry-vetted, and kept up-to-date with the latest practices.

Community Driven

Our learner community, forums, and live sessions create genuine peer-to-peer support.

Innovation Led

AI-powered recommendations, adaptive paths, and blockchain credentials push learning forward.

Our Journey

From Idea to Global Platform

Five years. One mission. A whole lot of learning.

2020
EduLearn Founded
Sarah and James launch EduLearn from a San Francisco garage with 12 courses and a vision to democratise education.
2021
100,000 Learners
Hit 100k enrolled students. Closed a $5M seed round led by Horizon Ventures. Expanded the team to 30 people.
2022
AI Learning Engine Launched
Introduced our adaptive AI engine, personalising learning paths for every student and increasing course completion rates by 47%.
2023
Went Global
Launched multi-language support in 12 languages. Crossed 500,000 learners across 30 countries. Series B of $25M.
2024 – Present
1.2 Million Learners & Growing
Now serving 1.2M+ learners in 40+ countries. Building EduLearn 2.0 with blockchain credentials and live cohort rooms.
